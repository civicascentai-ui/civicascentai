AI Opportunity & Skills Gap

The AI skills gap is growing.

AI adoption is moving quickly, while workers and communities still face a major need for practical training. CivicAscent AI focuses on helping beginners build the skills and confidence to participate.

59%of the global workforce is projected to need training by 2030.
67%of the North American workforce is projected to need training by 2030.
86%of employers expect AI and information-processing technologies to transform their business by 2030.
76%of surveyed knowledge workers said AI skills are needed to remain competitive in the job market.

What the data tells us

AI growth creates a training opportunity.

There is no single reliable statistic that classifies everyone as an “AI beginner.” Instead, this dashboard uses published workforce indicators to show the gap between rapid AI adoption, employer expectations, and the continuing need for skills development.
