# CivicAscent AI — Team Operational Order

## Shared mission
Build a visually exceptional, immersive, accessible AI experience that demonstrates practical AI power and supports a sustainable business.

## Agent 1 — Experience Director
Owns experience architecture, cinematic direction, interaction clarity, storytelling, motion, accessibility intent, and the Osmo-level interaction benchmark.
First operational contribution: the CivicAscent Central station concept uses the environment itself as navigation and gives visitors three immediately understandable destinations.

## Agent 2 — Lead Production Engineer
Owns implementation, architecture, browser/mobile behavior, performance, integrations, version control, deployment, and stability.
First operational contribution: wire each station destination to a real working CivicAscent capability rather than a dead concept page.

## Agent 3 — Quality & Accessibility Director
Owns independent release review, accessibility validation, navigation integrity, device/browser checks, performance acceptance, and first-time-user clarity.
First operational contribution: require keyboard access, large interaction targets, reduced-motion support, semantic navigation, readable contrast, and no forced audio.

## QC governance
No known defect may be carried forward. No agent self-approves final release. Research and Think Tank ideas are encouraged, but changes flow through Experience → Engineering → QA → QC oversight → Production.

## Automation enhancement rule
Automation ideas are adopted only when they improve clarity, usefulness, accessibility, or operational efficiency. The approved implementation order and safeguards are defined in `AUTOMATION_ENHANCEMENT_BLUEPRINT.md`. Every automation remains subject to the existing Experience → Engineering → QA → QC → Production path, the no-known-defect rule, security/privacy review, human escalation where appropriate, and the no-unapproved-paid-add-on rule.


## Automation maturity rule
CivicAscent uses the Automation Ladder defined in `AUTOMATION_ENHANCEMENT_BLUEPRINT.md`. Teams must use the lowest level that fully solves the problem. L5 requires governed-agent controls and explicit stop rules. L6 is a restricted exception for low-risk, reversible, observable tasks only and is not the default operating target.


## Marketing governance rule
CivicAscent marketing follows the controlled workflow defined in `MARKETING_OPERATING_MODEL.md`: Idea → AI Draft → Brand Review → Accessibility Review → Claims/Compliance Review → Preview → Human Approval → Publish → Analytics → Improve. AI-generated people/content must be labeled when required, fabricated testimonials and unsupported claims are prohibited, and no content may auto-publish without the required approval gate. Additional paid tools or APIs require separate approval.
