# CivicAscent AI — Learn → Build → Lead Pathway

Adopted: 2026-10-03
Status: Approved staging curriculum architecture

## Purpose
CivicAscent AI uses a three-level practical learning pathway that develops a learner from beginner understanding through implementation and into responsible AI adoption leadership.

## Level 1 — Learn: AI Foundations & Consulting
Outcome: understand a problem, assess where AI may help, communicate clearly, and recommend practical options without overpromising.

Core competencies:
- AI fundamentals and responsible use
- Prompting and conversational iteration
- Verification and source checking
- Needs assessment
- Problem framing
- Use-case selection
- Plain-language recommendations
- Privacy and safety basics

Practice examples:
- Interview a mock client or community partner
- Identify a workflow problem
- Compare AI and non-AI approaches
- Produce a short recommendation with assumptions and risks

## Level 2 — Build: AI Implementation
Outcome: turn an approved use case into a tested working prototype or workflow.

Core competencies:
- Workflow mapping
- Automation design
- Voice/assistant systems
- RAG and knowledge systems
- Integration fundamentals
- Human approval gates
- Security and privacy checks
- Testing and QA
- Documentation and handoff

Practice examples:
- Build a learner-inquiry automation
- Create a voice-assistant prototype
- Build a small governed knowledge workflow
- Test failure states and document corrections

## Level 3 — Lead: AI Adoption & Change Leadership
Outcome: help a team or community adopt AI responsibly and improve the system over time.

Core competencies:
- Change-management fundamentals
- Training and facilitation
- Stakeholder communication
- Responsible-use policy
- Privacy and security awareness
- Adoption support
- Measuring outcomes
- Feedback loops and continuous improvement

Practice examples:
- Build an adoption plan
- Teach a short beginner session
- Create a responsible-use guide
- Measure learner or team adoption
- Propose improvements based on feedback

## Entry Point — Free Workshop
The free workshop introduces practical AI, useful prompting, safer verification, and the Learn → Build → Lead pathway.

- Do not imply accreditation, professional certification, licensure, guaranteed employment, guaranteed contracts, or guaranteed income unless independently established and formally approved.

- All levels remain subject to CivicAscent accessibility, beginner clarity, security, privacy, factual-accuracy, and no-known-defect rules.
