# Veteran-Inclusive AI Literacy | Facilitator Pilot Guide v0.1

**Draft for independent veteran and accessibility review. Not the paid Facilitator course.**

## Session preparation
Duration: 60 minutes. Audience: adults with any or no military background. Materials: the Starter pilot lesson, accessible text worksheet, optional internet-connected device, and paper alternatives. Do not require an AI account or purchase to participate. Prepare a fictional resume example and verify external links in advance.

## Timed run of show
| Minutes | Segment | Facilitator action | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | Welcome and consent | State voluntary participation and privacy rules; invite preferred participation method | Participant knows how to opt out |
| 5–15 | AI strengths and limits | Demonstrate a fictional work task and identify a hallucination risk | Learner identifies a claim to verify |
| 15–25 | Privacy | Show how to replace personal details with fictional examples | Learner identifies two prohibited data types |
| 25–40 | Career translation | Compare AI-generated resume bullets against the fictional source; delete invented achievements | Learner revises one bullet accurately |
| 40–50 | Verify output | Compare an AI-generated claim to an official job posting or trusted source | Learner records a verification source |
| 50–60 | Reflection and feedback | Offer spoken, typed or written responses; collect only consented feedback | Anonymous rubric, no service-history data |

## Facilitator script: opening
`This session is an introduction to using AI critically. AI can help draft ideas, but it can be wrong. You decide what is accurate and what to share. You do not need to disclose military service, medical details, benefits information or anything private. You may participate without using an AI tool.`

## Demonstration prompt
`You are helping with a fictional operations coordinator resume. Using only the experience below, draft three short accomplishment-neutral bullets. Do not invent metrics, awards, licenses or leadership scope. If information is missing, ask a question rather than guessing. Experience: Coordinated a six-person team, scheduled equipment inspections, and documented readiness checks.`

## Facilitator safety and inclusion
- Never request sensitive military, medical, benefits or identity information.
- Allow additional time, keyboard-only navigation, large text and plain-text copies; do not assume captions substitute for ASL.
- Do not diagnose, counsel, interpret VA eligibility or promise jobs or certificates.
- Invite corrections from participants without pressuring disclosure of lived experience.
- If an accessibility barrier appears, stop that exercise and offer an equivalent alternative; log the issue for remediation.

## Assessment rubric
Rate each outcome Demonstrated / Needs practice / Not assessed: (1) identifies AI limitation; (2) removes sensitive data; (3) detects invented resume claim; (4) verifies a job-related claim. An unassessed learner is not a failure. No individual performance records without explicit informed consent.

## After-action review
Record session date, version, facilitator, total anonymous participant count, accessibility issues, inaccurate statements, remediation owner and retest result. Do not record service status, disability or medical details. Obtain explicit consent before quoting participant feedback.

## Acceptance gate
Independent veteran-serving reviewer and disabled-learner accessibility tester must review before external promotion. This pilot guide does not authorize course sales, SkillBridge affiliation or VA approval.
