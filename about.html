ABOUT CIVICASCENT AI

Practical AI learning for real life.

CivicAscent AI develops approachable artificial intelligence and digital-skills learning for adults, older learners, veterans, families, community groups, and organizations.

Who is behind CivicAscent AI?

James K. Peters leads CivicAscent AI. We focus on clear instruction, hands-on learning, safety, accessibility, and helping people use new technology with confidence.

Our work serves the Fort Worth, Texas area and community partners. Workshop offerings, dates, language availability, and format must be confirmed before you register.

How we teach

We demonstrate a practical task, let you try it, help you check the result, and show how to improve it. No coding experience is required to start.
