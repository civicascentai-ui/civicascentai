# CivicAscent AI partnership proposal | updated working draft | 2026-10-11

**Status: working proposal, not a confirmed partnership, grant award, nonprofit determination, or live product.**

## Purpose
CivicAscent AI proposes an accessible, veteran-led introductory AI learning pilot for adults, including veterans, older adults, workforce learners and Spanish-speaking participants in Fort Worth/Tarrant County. Facilitation draws on James K Peters's veteran service and information-technology operations and management experience.

## Revised delivery model
**Primary offering: secure online classroom.** Learners sign in to access a structured, continuously improved curriculum on CivicAscent's website. Lessons may include beginner-friendly text, practical activities, responsible AI and verification exercises, accessibility features, progress tracking, and optional approved companion handouts. CivicAscent can grant, extend or revoke future access through its entitlement system. Already-downloaded handouts cannot be recalled. New classroom capabilities are under QA development, not yet commercially launched.

## Proposed small pilot
Four sessions for approximately 15–25 learners, tailored to a partner's population and accessibility needs. Potential learning topics: practical prompting, fact-checking, privacy, workplace communication, and real-world projects. A separate working funding concept is a $5,000 bilingual pilot for up to 30 adults and four free workshops; this is a proposal amount, not secured funding. Final capacity, dates, language support, staffing and curriculum remain subject to agreement.

## Scholarship and sponsored access program (proposed, not yet operational)
The scholarship provides **tuition-free, time-limited classroom access** to approved learners without requiring a paid purchase. Scholarships may be funded by grants, donations, sponsors or CivicAscent-designated seats when capacity permits. The program must not promise funding that has not been received.

**Application:** simple accessible form with contact method, requested course, optional language/accessibility support, and concise need statement. Avoid unnecessary sensitive personal information. Do not require veteran status as a condition unless a particular restricted sponsor award legally requires it.

**Decision:** published eligibility criteria, capacity and review period; staff review without automated discriminatory decisions. Maintain an auditable award/decline/waitlist outcome and appeal or correction channel. No application fees.

**Award:** verified learner account receives a scholarship entitlement with start/end dates and course scope. No Stripe charge and no fake paid ledger row. A scholarship is distinct from a purchase, sponsor pledge or refund.

**Administration:** least-privilege staff can grant, extend, suspend and revoke future course access with reasons and audit records. Expired/revoked accounts are denied by server-side checks. No cross-account sharing.

**Fairness and privacy:** clear privacy notice, minimal retention, accessible mobile form, optional bilingual support, and human review for exceptions. Under-18 learners require appropriate guardian and program safeguarding procedures.

**Capacity:** show actual funded or authorized seat inventory; waitlist when full; do not allocate unfunded sponsored seats. Report awards and learning outcomes in aggregate without exposing learner identities.

## Partnership request
Request an exploratory meeting to discuss pilot host facilities, referrals, connectivity, loaner devices, volunteers, scholarship sponsorship, and evaluation. Partners are prospective until they affirm participation in writing.

## Current readiness and release gates
Classroom prototype is being built in isolated QA. Payment/refund access revocation, authenticated lesson delivery, mobile CORS, scholarship authorization and learner privacy need independent QA. Production release remains HOLD until 25/25 independently verified E2E scenarios and human approval.
