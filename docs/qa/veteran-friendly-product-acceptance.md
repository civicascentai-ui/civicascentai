# Veteran-Friendly Product Acceptance

Status: DESIGN SPECIFICATION, NOT AN IMPLEMENTED OR ENDORSED VETERAN PROGRAM.

## What the offer actually provides
A practical AI-literacy learning pathway for veterans, military families and transitioning service members: everyday AI literacy, resume and interview assistance, translating military experience into civilian skills, small-business and workforce applications, privacy, verification and safe use. Materials should not require disclosure of service history, disability, or benefits status.

## Product scope
- Starter: foundational AI tools, source-checking, privacy, job-search practice and a take-home worksheet.
- Facilitator: all foundational objectives plus instructor lesson plans, accessible exercises, group facilitation and participant evaluation.
- Clearly label what is and is not included; do not imply government certification, VA approval, GI Bill eligibility, veteran discounts or official partnership without documented authorization.
- Provide plain-language learning objectives, optional examples for military-to-civilian transitions, flexible pacing, captions/transcripts, keyboard-accessible exercises and non-military alternatives.
- Avoid claims of therapeutic, medical, legal or VA-benefits expertise. Refer specialized benefits or crisis questions to qualified services rather than giving definitive advice.

## QC / acceptance
- [ ] At least one veteran or veteran-serving subject-matter reviewer assesses relevance and respectful terminology.
- [ ] Independent accessibility QA includes disabled-veteran use cases without collecting sensitive status data.
- [ ] Sample lesson, worksheet, facilitator guide and accurate marketing description reviewed and approved.
- [ ] Confirm support route, refund terms, course access and all checkout-to-delivery steps.
- [ ] Document reviewer consent and approval for any testimonial or partner logo.

## Marketing rule
Until reviews pass, use `Veteran-inclusive curriculum in development` rather than `VA-approved`, `veteran-certified`, `veteran-tested`, or `fully accessible`. No production advertising changes until approved.
