# Accessibility and ASL Acceptance Plan

Status: REVIEW PLAN, NOT A WCAG OR ASL CERTIFICATION. QA only.

## WCAG 2.2 AA review matrix (also covers relevant 2.1 AA requirements)
- [ ] Keyboard-only navigation, visible focus, no keyboard traps; dialogs and menus return focus correctly.
- [ ] Screen reader headings, landmarks, meaningful link names, form labels, required/error states, announcements.
- [ ] Contrast for text and UI components; text resize/reflow at 200%/400%, responsive mobile zoom.
- [ ] Alt text for meaningful images; decorative imagery ignored; charts and diagrams have equivalent descriptions.
- [ ] Captions for prerecorded video, transcripts for audio, audio descriptions or equivalent for visual-only instruction where needed.
- [ ] Authentication, OTP and checkout do not rely on inaccessible puzzles or memory-only tasks.
- [ ] Errors prevent data loss, are clearly described and have recovery instructions.
- [ ] Course ZIP contents, documents and exercises are included in audit, not just the marketing website.

## ASL track
ASL is not interchangeable with captions or written English. Do not claim ASL support until qualified Deaf/ASL reviewers validate content and navigation. Prioritize an ASL orientation, purchase/access instructions and learner support contact information. Provide accessible captions/transcripts independently. Seek voluntary feedback from Deaf community testers with informed consent; do not advertise donated services as committed until accepted.

## Independent testing evidence
For each issue capture page/asset version, browser/device, assistive technology, WCAG criterion, steps, observed result, severity, owner, fix commit, retest date and tester approval. Include at least one keyboard-only and one screen-reader tester; recruit Deaf/ASL reviewers for ASL claims. Keep personal data out of bug screenshots.

## Release policy
Any blocker preventing payment, sign-in, essential course access or completion for disabled learners is a launch HOLD. Publish an accurate accessibility statement with known limitations and a working contact route only after those routes are verified.
