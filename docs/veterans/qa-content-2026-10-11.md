# Veterans and Military Families: QA-only information draft

Status: INTERNAL DEVELOPMENT ONLY. No enrollment, payments, funded seats or public launch.

CivicAscent AI is developing a beginner-friendly 90-minute, veteran-led AI workforce-readiness workshop. The proposed facilitator is the president, a military veteran with prior IT operations and business management experience. Facilitation rehearsal, backup support and independent testing remain open.

Proposed learning activities: (1) identify transferable skills using a fictional scenario; (2) draft and fact-check a civilian resume statement; (3) compare a fictional job description with verified skills; (4) practice safe workplace planning; (5) complete a five-question knowledge check and identify a next step.

Privacy: default to fictional examples; no service, health, financial, identity or confidential workplace records in public AI tools. Paper exercises and no-personal-account participation are proposed, not yet verified.

Accessibility: before enrollment, verify a private human contact for accommodation requests, readable accessible materials, captions where appropriate, keyboard and screen-reader paths, and actual ability to deliver approved requests. No interpreter or language service is promised.

Claims: no VA, GI Bill or DoD SkillBridge approval or endorsement; no guaranteed employment or placement; no scholarships or free seats are currently available. Historical $0 draft copy was corrected; a reported provisional $49 draft is not approved tuition. No purchase link or registration form should be shown.
