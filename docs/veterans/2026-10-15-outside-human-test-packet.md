## One-to-one outside user task session (target 30–45 minutes each)
A learner may use a printed packet or accessible digital copy. No personal AI account, purchase, or upload is required.

1. **Start and orientation (5 min):** facilitator explains fictional-only data, no official veteran benefit affiliation; observer checks if learner can state the purpose and find a pause/ask-for-help path without prompting.
2. **Recognize facts (5 min):** imaginary pantry story: a volunteer arranged weekly donations, maintained an inventory sheet and worked with six volunteers. The pantry met the scheduled deliveries. Ask for two actual skills demonstrated. Mark only facts from the story, not invented medals/ranks.
3. **Draft and fact-check (10 min):** learner edits a sample sentence: "Certified logistics supervisor who improved distribution by 40%." Ask them to remove the unsupported qualification and percentage and produce one checkable sentence about coordination and inventory. Offline rewriting is equally valid.
4. **Match a fictional job description (5 min):** fictional *Warehouse Assistant* job asks for keeping inventory records, coordinating delivery schedules, and communicating with coworkers. Learner circles one supported match and one detail they would need to confirm; do not invent a qualification.
5. **Privacy knowledge (5 min):** learner identifies three data categories not to type into a public chatbot, and chooses a fictional practice example rather than personal military/disability records.
6. **Close and accessibility feedback (5–10 min):** offer the five-question check from the current guide, a one-week realistic next action and optional anonymous feedback about pace/clarity/print size/controls. Record defects in a secure location. No recording without separate affirmative opt-in.

**Core success:** learner identifies two grounded skills, produces one fact-checked artifact, rejects invented credentials/metrics, distinguishes fictional from sensitive data, and finds the relevant help or offline option. Original source's learning check target remains >=4/5 plus one reviewed artifact. Record BOTH task completion and assessment separately. A target of at least 4/5 learner sessions reaching core goals is a *review threshold*, never a substitute for fixing serious barriers.
