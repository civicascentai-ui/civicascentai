# CivicAscent AI — Level 2 — Practical AI Prompts and Workflows
Status: CURRICULUM SOURCE / REVIEW DRAFT. Saved to isolated branch only; no website changes, no learner publication.
Audience: beginner-friendly, older adults, veterans and other adult learners. Accessibility: one idea per card, plain English, suitable for large-print/mobile study.

## Card 1
Front: What is a clear AI prompt?
Back: A request that names the task, context, and desired output.

## Card 2
Front: Why give an AI a role?
Back: It helps shape the style and focus of the response.

## Card 3
Front: What does a goal statement do?
Back: Defines the exact result you want.

## Card 4
Front: Why include examples?
Back: They show what a useful response should look like.

## Card 5
Front: What is a workflow?
Back: A sequence of steps from request to result.

## Card 6
Front: What is a handoff?
Back: Passing the task, context, and next action to another helper.

## Card 7
Front: When should you check AI facts?
Back: Whenever an answer affects an important decision.

## Card 8
Front: What should stay out of prompts?
Back: Passwords and other confidential personal information.

## Card 9
Front: What does /test mean?
Back: Check the result against clear success criteria.

## Card 10
Front: What does /status report?
Back: Progress, evidence, blockers, and the next step.
