# CivicAscent AI — Veterans learning adaptation, no-cost pilot supplement
2026-10-14 · Status: review draft; no enrollment, no verified independent veteran field test.

## Goal
Give veterans, transitioning service members, and military-family adults optional learning examples without assuming everyone has the same career history, abilities or benefits eligibility.

## Three safe exercises
1. **Skills translation:** Using a completely fictional role (e.g., supply coordinator), draft three civilian-facing bullet points describing planning, inventory and teamwork. Do not upload DD214 or real service records; review every claim.
2. **Interview practice:** Ask an AI tool to act as a neutral interviewer for a fictional administrative job. Give constructive, plain-language feedback; do not promise interviews, VA approval or employment outcomes.
3. **Privacy & source check:** Mark these statements “requires verification”: “You qualify for X benefit”; “The program is VA approved”; “A workshop guarantees employment.” Find the appropriate official human resource for verification, without offering eligibility advice.

## Pilot outline (90 minutes)
Welcome and consent (10); plain-language introduction (15); privacy and fact-checking (15); skills translation practice (20); safe mock interview (20); feedback and questions (10). Provide large print, keyboard-accessible options, transcripts/captions if multimedia is used, non-digital alternate activities.

## Approval gates
FRIDAY reviews accessibility and comprehension; outside *human* veteran reviewers provide opt-in feedback; partner permission is documented before hosting; program and privacy statements are checked. Until then report "Prepared for review, pilot not delivered." This supplement does not replace the assembled internal Veterans Training Package V3 or the original source documents in issue #36.
