### Draft 02 | Three-step prompt framework | LinkedIn/Facebook
> One way to improve an AI answer: describe the goal, add useful context and specify the format.
>
> Instead of “Help with a meeting,” try:
>
> “I’m preparing for a 20-minute introductory meeting with a community nonprofit. Using only the information I provide, draft five respectful questions in a numbered list. Mark anything you cannot verify.”
>
> Then read the answer carefully. AI can still invent details, so verify names, dates and claims before sharing.
>
> One better question can be a useful first skill.
>
> #ResponsibleAI #AILearning
