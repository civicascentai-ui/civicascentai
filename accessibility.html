Accessibility

CivicAscent AI is designed to make AI understandable and usable for people with different levels of technical experience and different access needs.

Our website standard

  • Keyboard-accessible navigation and visible focus states
  • Readable text and strong color contrast
  • Meaningful alternative text for informative images
  • Support for browser text enlargement and responsive mobile layouts
  • Reduced-motion behavior for people who request it in their device settings
  • Clear labels, instructions, errors, and next actions
  • Captions or text alternatives when meaningful audio or video is introduced

Report an accessibility problem

If something prevents you from using the site, email info@civicascentai.com. Include the page and a short description of the problem.
