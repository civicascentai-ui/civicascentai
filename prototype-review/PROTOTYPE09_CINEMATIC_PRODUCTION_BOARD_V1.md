# Prototype 09 — Cinematic Review v1 Production Board

Status: REVIEW CANDIDATE / NOT PRODUCTION
Theme: Safari Learning World
Goal: Make CivicAscent AI feel like a directed short film with an interactive layer.

## User Outcome
A person new to AI should immediately understand:
1. where they are;
2. what they can do;
3. which interaction to try first;
4. what AI can help them accomplish;
5. how to continue without feeling lost.

## Scene 01 — Arrival at the Safari Lodge
Purpose: Establish place, trust, wonder, and one obvious next action.

Visual:
- premium African safari environment at golden hour;
- elevated lodge/terrace foreground framing;
- watering hole in middle distance;
- elephants as dominant living event;
- restrained giraffe/bird movement;
- atmospheric haze, water highlights, grasses, warm directional light.

Camera:
- slow 4–6 second push from terrace into the landscape;
- minimal parallax from lodge foreground;
- no shake, no gaming movement;
- mobile crop preserves lodge edge, elephants, and Start Here interaction.

Interaction:
- one primary environmental hotspot: START HERE;
- two secondary destinations visible but visually quieter;
- primary action opens the first guided AI scene.

Accessibility:
- large text and 48px+ touch targets;
- keyboard focus;
- visible text equivalent for voice;
- reduced-motion mode freezes camera push and ambient motion;
- no essential information hidden in animation.

## Scene 02 — AI Learning Trail
Purpose: Demonstrate one practical AI capability without jargon.

Story beat:
Visitor asks a simple question in plain language.
The experience shows AI turning it into a clearer, useful answer.

Camera/transition:
- soft environmental reframe rather than a hard page cut;
- user maintains orientation to the Safari world;
- clear Return to Safari control.

## Scene 03 — Living AI Lab
Purpose: Show creation from a rough idea.

Story beat:
A rough idea becomes a structured plan, lesson, visual concept, or useful document.

Direction:
- cinematic reveal of the transformation;
- no dashboard/card-grid treatment;
- one result at a time.

## Scene 04 — Accessible AI
Purpose: Demonstrate that AI can be usable by people with different abilities and experience levels.

Story beat:
Show voice, readable text, clear controls, reduced-motion option, and plain-language guidance as part of the scene rather than a settings lecture.

## Storyboard — Review v1
Shot 1: Wide lodge reveal. Golden-hour safari, lodge framing foreground, elephants at watering hole.
Shot 2: Slow push toward watering hole. Start Here hotspot becomes subtly prominent.
Shot 3: User activates Start Here. Environment darkens slightly around focal route, not full-screen overlay.
Shot 4: Guided AI demonstration appears integrated into the world with one clear prompt/result sequence.
Shot 5: Transition to Living AI Lab, using environmental reframe and light movement rather than generic modal animation.
Shot 6: Return to lodge with three destinations visible, clearly showing what to do next.

## Motion Layers
1. camera drift / push;
2. elephant and secondary wildlife movement;
3. water reflection;
4. grass and foliage movement;
5. haze/light breathing;
6. hotspot pulse only when action is available.

No simultaneous attention-grabbing motion from every layer.

## Voice / Sound
- calm human guide;
- concise phrases;
- optional voice;
- visible text always present;
- environmental ambience can be added later only after visual prototype passes.

## Mobile
- preserve focal event;
- avoid tiny environmental labels;
- primary hotspot moves to clear thumb-accessible position;
- headline remains readable without covering wildlife;
- secondary hotspots may stack near lower half while remaining scene-integrated.

## Review Gate
Review v1 passes only if:
- looks like a directed scene, not a webpage;
- obvious next action;
- Safari focal event is dominant;
- desktop and mobile both preserve scene hierarchy;
- no known accessibility blocker;
- no card-grid drift;
- no unrelated retired theme appears.

## Implementation Order

4. Agent 3 accessibility/mobile review.
5. Agent 4 cinematic review.
6. Fix all blockers.
