FIVE-LESSON PATHWAY

Confidence, built step by step.

1. Meet ChatGPT

Understand conversational AI and why important facts still need verification.

2. Prompting Without Fear

3. Conversation Is the Superpower

4. Voice & Accessibility

Use voice or dictation when available while protecting private information.

WHAT LEARNERS CAN DEMONSTRATE

Completion should lead to usable skills.

Prompt clearly

State a goal, context, constraints and desired output.

Improve an answer

Use follow-up questions to refine a weak first response.

Verify important claims

Recognize when outside checking or a trusted source is needed.

Complete a real task

Use AI to support planning, writing, research or organization while keeping human judgment in control.
