Privacy and Contact

Draft notice for public-site quality review. Last updated October 10, 2026.

Contact

For program questions, registration inquiries, accessibility requests, or privacy questions, email civicascentai@gmail.com.

Information you choose to share

When you contact us by email, we receive the information you send, such as your email address and message. Please do not email passwords, payment card numbers, medical records, government identifiers, or other sensitive information.

Registration and payments

The public information site currently offers email inquiries about programs, not confirmed enrollment or payment checkout. Paid enrollment is temporarily unavailable. Do not send payment information by email.

Website services

Website hosting and your email provider may process technical information needed to deliver pages or messages. This notice does not claim that no logs, cookies, or third-party processing exist. The final production configuration and any embedded third-party services must be reviewed before publication.

Questions and requests

Contact us at the address above to ask about information you previously sent or request a different way to access program information. We will review requests and respond as appropriate.

Publication gate: Verify actual production data practices, retention, hosting and any analytics or embedded services before approving this notice for public release.
