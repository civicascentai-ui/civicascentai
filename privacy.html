Privacy and your information

Updated October 16, 2026. CivicAscent AI wants you to know what information a workshop inquiry may request before you choose to submit it.

Before publication: This draft must be checked against current form settings, account practices, provider terms, and applicable requirements. Do not publish unverified commitments.

Information you choose to provide

Workshop registration may ask for your name, email address, AI experience, and the topics you want to learn. A separate question or contact form may ask for information you voluntarily write. Please do not provide passwords, payment-card numbers, financial records, medical information, or other sensitive details in optional text fields.

Why we ask

Information you provide in a registration or inquiry is intended to help with workshop planning, responding to questions, and determining learning interests. Submitting an inquiry does not by itself guarantee a workshop seat.

Third-party registration

When you select our free workshop registration link, you leave our website for a Stripe-hosted form. The form provider may process the information you supply under its own terms and privacy notice. You can review the provider's policies before submitting details. Workshop registration is advertised as $0; its live field requirements and confirmation process should be verified before any public release of this notice.

Storage, sharing and retention

Retention periods, access permissions, and any additional processors must be documented and verified against CivicAscent AI's actual operations before this notice is finalized. Do not rely on this draft as a complete statement of our data practices.

Questions or requests about information

For questions about information you submitted or to request assistance with a record, contact civicascentai@gmail.com. We may need to verify that the request relates to your information before acting on it.
