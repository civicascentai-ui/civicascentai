Questions & answers

I'm new to AI. Can I still participate?

Yes. Our beginner-oriented learning plans are designed to introduce useful AI ideas without requiring technical experience. Specific classes and enrollment dates have not yet been confirmed.

Do I need to pay to ask a question?

No. Sending an interest inquiry or asking a question costs nothing. This page is separate from all paid products and does not accept payments.

Is this a confirmed course registration?

No. An inquiry does not reserve a seat or grant access to a course. CivicAscent AI will separately confirm any available learning opportunities and terms.

What subjects is CivicAscent AI exploring?

AI basics, useful prompts, everyday and workplace tasks, fact-checking, and responsible handling of private information. We are also developing learning approaches for community organizations.

Is this government-approved or a job guarantee?

No. Do not interpret our learning information as VA, GI Bill, SkillBridge, government certification, guaranteed employment, or an official partnership.

Should I share personal information in an AI exercise?

Use fictional examples whenever possible. Do not include passwords, identity numbers, sensitive service records, medical information, or confidential employer documents in public AI systems.
