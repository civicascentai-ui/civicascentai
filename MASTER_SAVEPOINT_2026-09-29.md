## Active direction
Prototype 09 — Safari Learning World.

The approved direction is a cinematic, premium, living African safari environment viewed from an elevated lodge/terrace. Elephants at the watering hole remain the dominant living event. Giraffes, birds, grasses, water reflections, atmospheric haze, and subtle environmental motion support the scene without competing for attention.

The experience must remain:
- immersive and environmental, not a brochure or card grid;
- mobile-first and accessible;
- easy for first-time AI users and older users to understand;
- visually rich but simple in attention;
- controlled in motion, with one dominant story;
- performance-conscious with reduced-motion support;
- protected by the no-known-defect rule.
