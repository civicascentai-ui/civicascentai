HOW LEVEL 2 WORKS

A guided path from goal to demonstrated skill.

1. Define the goal

Choose a specific skill, work task or learning objective.

2. Build the roadmap

Break the goal into manageable lessons, practice and checkpoints.

3. Apply it

Complete practical exercises and a real-world project tied to the goal.

4. Review progress

Use feedback and checkpoints to identify what is working and what comes next.

Example Level 2 project

A learner might build a complete AI-assisted workflow for a real need—such as researching a topic, organizing findings, drafting a useful deliverable, checking important claims and documenting the final human review.

Completion evidence

Designated Level 2 completions can include a CivicAscent AI Certificate of Completion and a summary of the learner's completed project or demonstrated skills. The certificate is a CivicAscent training credential, not academic credit, a professional license or third-party accreditation.
