# CivicAscent clear-writing QC

Use this locally defined skill for website copy, presentations, scripts, proposals, outreach drafts, and learning materials. It implements our own editorial approach; it does not install the Stop Slop vendor skill.

## Procedure

1. State the audience and communication goal in one sentence.
2. Retain verifiable factual claims, concrete dates, prices, commitments, correct credentials, and all mandatory disclosures. Do not imply tax exemption, 501(c)(3), partner acceptance, or course completion without evidence.
3. Replace vague claims, generic filler, redundancy, unnatural phrasing, and jargon with concise, direct language.
4. Verify every program promise against available company records and mark unknowns for review.
5. For learner-facing text, favor plain language and accessibility. Provide equivalent Spanish content when used for bilingual workflows.
6. Compare before/after meaning and confirm legal/compliance notices, refund terms, data practices, and alt text have not been weakened.
7. Return suggested wording with a brief QC result and unresolved claims. Drafts do not publish themselves.

## Constraints

Never send messages, claim certification or government approval, remove required fine print, or promise participant outcomes. LPC review remains a mandatory gate for high-risk legal, funding, or tax representations.
